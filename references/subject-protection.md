# Subject Protection

## General

Protect the core story, recognizable primary subject, and key relationships.

Do not mechanically preserve every object or detail.

## Portraits

Prioritize:

- identity
- face
- pose
- major clothing structure
- overall body proportions

Allowed:

- subtle beautification
- natural skin-tone optimization
- reconstruction of non-core surroundings

Do not let the background merge visually with the person.

Do not let collage elements obscure the face or damage recognizability.

## Food

Prioritize:

- the food itself
- quantity relationships
- core plating
- major ingredients
- recognizable presentation

The following may be substantially reconstructed:

- tabletop
- walls
- background
- decorative environment

Do not arbitrarily redesign the core food simply to achieve a stronger style.

## Landscapes

When there is no single dominant subject, protect key relationships.

For example:

> architecture + tree + water

does not need to be copied object by object, but the relationship should remain.

## Night Scenes

Protect:

- core subject
- light-source relationships
- light/dark structure
- water and reflections when important
- nighttime atmosphere

Dark areas may become useful spaces for environmental reconstruction, but the core subject must remain visible.

## Final Check

After generation, verify:

1. Is the original story still recognizable?
2. Is the core subject intact?
3. Is the subject still the visual focus?
4. Has the environment actually been reconstructed?
5. Do collage elements serve the subject?
6. Does the result still feel derived from the original photograph?
